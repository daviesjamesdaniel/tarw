# Privacy Policy

Tarw is a local, native email client. It does not have a backend server, and its developer does not collect, receive, or store any of your data.

## What Tarw accesses

When you sign in with Gmail via Google OAuth2, Tarw requests access to your Gmail mailbox (read, send, and manage messages) so it can act as an email client for your account. This access:

- Happens entirely between your device and Google/your mail provider's own servers — nothing passes through, or is visible to, the developer.
- Is used only to display and manage your own mail within the app, exactly like any other email client (Thunderbird, Apple Mail, Gmail's own app, etc.).
- Is never sold, shared, or used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than running the app.

## Where your data is stored

- OAuth tokens and app passwords are stored in your operating system's own secure credential store (e.g. GNOME Keyring, KWallet), never in plain text, never uploaded anywhere.
- A local mail cache (message headers, for offline browsing) is stored only on your own device.

## Third parties

Tarw itself involves no third parties. Your mail provider (Google, Fastmail, iCloud, etc.) has its own privacy policy governing your account with them.

## Source code

Tarw is [free and open-source software](https://github.com/daviesjamesdaniel/tarw) — anyone can inspect exactly what the app does with your data.
